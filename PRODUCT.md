## Product Principles

1. The work leads; the interface recedes. Every effect must reveal a decision, a detail or an artifact.
2. Show thinking, not adjectives: decisions, alternatives, constraints and what changed.
3. Honest status over impressive status: launched, concept and course work are labelled as such.
4. One deep case study beats many shallow cards.
5. Usable first: keyboard, touch, reduced motion and slow connections get the complete experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, no hover-only essentials, comfortable touch targets, full reduced-motion experience, meaningful alt text for every project image.
